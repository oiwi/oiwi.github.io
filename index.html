About Me

I am a Principal Investigator at the University of Oxford, based in Information Engineering and the Oxford Robotics Institute (ORI), where I lead the Parker Jones Neural Processing Lab (PNPL).

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and neuroscience, with a particular focus on brain data, speech, and non-invasive brain-computer interfaces.
