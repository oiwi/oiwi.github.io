About Me

I am a Research Fellow in Information Engineering at the University of Oxford and a Principal Investigator in the Oxford Robotics Institute, where I lead the Parker Jones Neural Processing Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and neuroscience, with a particular focus on brain data, speech and non-invasive brain-computer interfaces.

Research

My group develops machine-learning methods for understanding and decoding neural signals, including MEG and fMRI, and builds large-scale datasets and benchmarks for brain data.
